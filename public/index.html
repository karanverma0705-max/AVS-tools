INDUSTRIAL METALLURGY

The Engineering Behind AVS Tools

Why automotive technicians, fabrication shops, and power plants choose AVS drop-forged tools over standard hardware.

Closed-Die Drop Forging

Alloy billets heated to 1250°C and hammered between precision dies. This continuous metal grain structure yields up to 300% higher tensile strength compared to brittle cast tools.

  • Zero internal porosity or micro-voids
  • High resistance to catastrophic fracturing under torque

Induction-Hardened Jaws

High-frequency induction coils selectively temper cutting edges and gripping teeth up to 64 HRC, maintaining razor sharpness while keeping the tool body ductile and shock absorbent.

  • Cuts piano wire, spring steel, and hardened cables
  • Precision cross-hatched non-slip teeth

Multi-Layer Anti-Corrosion

Satin chrome plating, manganese phosphating, and clear passivated coats seal tool surfaces against moisture, motor oil, grease, brake fluid, and harsh workshop environments.

  • Salt-spray tested to 240+ hours
  • Anti-glare finish for bright shop lights

Ergonomic Anti-Fatigue Grips

Dual-compound thermoplastic elastomers (TPR) and heavy-dip vinyl grips contoured to the human palm. Includes safety slip-stops and dielectric insulation up to 500V.

  • High dielectric puncture resistance
  • Shock-reduction and oil-resistant grip
